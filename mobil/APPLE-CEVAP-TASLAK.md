**1. Screen recording**

**2. Purpose and target audience**
Tetikte is an exam preparation app for candidates of the Turkish professional accountancy exams: the SMMM internship entrance exam (SGS) and the SMMM qualification exam. Users solve multiple-choice practice questions; every question has a step-by-step explanation and a reference to the relevant law or accounting standard, and users track their progress by topic. Candidates usually study from scattered PDFs and outdated question sets; Tetikte gives them current, topic-organised questions on mobile, including offline use. Audience: adults (18+) in Türkiye preparing for these exams.

**3. How to access the main features**
- Launch the app. The "Ücretsiz" (Free) tab opens sample questions; 3 questions can be solved without an account.
- To see all features, open the "Hesap" (Account) tab → "Giriş yap" (Log in) and use the demo account in the Sign-in Information section (destek@tetikte.com).
- After login: "Sınavlar" (Exams) → choose an exam → solve questions; each answer shows an explanation. "Karnem" shows progress.
- Account deletion: "Hesap" tab → "Hesabımı sil" (Delete my account) → confirm. The account and its data are deleted from our server immediately.
- The app is in Turkish.

**4. External services**
- Supabase — database and user authentication (hosts the question content)
- Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In — optional login methods
- Cloudflare Turnstile — bot protection on sign-up/login
- Resend — sends account e-mails (password reset)
- AI: practice questions are prepared by our team with AI-assisted drafting and automated checks before publication. The app itself does not call any AI service at runtime.
There is no payment processor in the iOS app.

**5. Regional differences**
The app is distributed only in Türkiye and works the same for all users.

**6. Regulated industry / third-party material**
Tetikte is an educational practice tool, not a financial or legal service. It is not affiliated with TÜRMOB, TESMER or any exam authority and does not use official exam papers. The questions are original, written by our company (Dizdar Denetim Danışmanlık ve Yazılım A.Ş.) with reference to publicly available Turkish legislation and published accounting and auditing standards.

**Paid content (Guideline 3.1.1)**
The iOS app contains no in-app purchases and no paid content. All content available in the iOS app is free. The iOS app does not unlock any content purchased outside the app, and it contains no links or calls to action for external purchases.
